## Abstract

Multilingual Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) systems are vital for enabling inclusive, voice-driven applications across global linguistic contexts. However, challenges such as code-switching and low-resource language processing limit their performance. This research presents a deep learning-based framework that integrates Language Identification (LID) directly into the ASR pipeline using a joint multitask learning approach. By sharing acoustic and contextual features across both tasks, the model improves transcription accuracy and language classification simultaneously. A custom multilingual dataset was created and pre-processed with spectrogram generation, normalization, and data augmentation. Three architectures— LID-only, ASR-only, and Joint LID+ASR— were trained and evaluated. Results show that the joint model achieved superior performance, with 91% LID accuracy, 12.1% Word Error Rate (WER), and 5.8% Character Error Rate (CER), outperforming standalone baselines. These findings demonstrate the effectiveness of integrating LID into ASR, especially in multilingual and noisy environments. The joint model offers a scalable, efficient solution for real-world speech applications. Future work will explore adaptation to unseen languages, handling intra-sentence code-switching, and optimizing for low latency deployment."
