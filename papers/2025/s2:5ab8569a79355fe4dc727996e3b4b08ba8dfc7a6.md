## Abstract

We propose a superframe-driven video summarization framework combining ResNet-50 CNN features, Deep Embedded Clustering (DEC), and Bi-LSTM with self-attention mechanisms for keyframe selection, integrated with a multilingual ASR–summarization–translation pipeline. Our method is evaluated on TVSum (50 videos), SumMe (25 videos), OVP (150 videos), and a custom educational dataset (75 lecture videos). The framework achieves F-scores of 0.687 on TVSum and 0.561 on SumMe, representing 8.4% and 6.7% improvements over state-of-the-art methods. Precision of 0.72 and recall of 0.65 demonstrate balanced performance, while maintaining 15:1 compression ratio and temporal coherence of 0.78. The multilingual module integrates Whisper ASR (4.2% WER), BART summarization (ROUGE-L 0.441), and mBART-50 translation, achieving 94.3% average BLEU score across five Indian languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi). Processing efficiency of 45 seconds for 10-minute videos on RTX 3090 enables practical deployment.
