## Abstract

Innovative language translation has become a need in the midst of the global demand for multilingual accessibility. "LinguaFusion: "AI-Powered Multilingual Video Voice Translator – A Seamless System Using State-of-the-Art Artificial Intelligence Technologies for Multilingual Voice Translation in Video Content" introduces an advanced system combining state of the art artificial intelligence (AI) technologies for the seamless multilingual voice translation in video content. It is built upon Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) to give accurate speech to text conversion, Neural Machine Translation (NMT) for translation with correct meaning and in situ context, and Text to speech (TTS) for natural and localised audio synthesis. LinguaFusion has been designed for real time operation, so that content creators can deliver videos that are accessible and inclusive, by localising across many different sets of linguistic and cultural audiences. What makes this project unique is the.uni ed AI driven architecture, the real time tags, plus customization features like voice accent and tonal adjustments. LinguaFusion bridges language gaps to create opportunities for sharing global content and stimulate cross cultural awareness.
