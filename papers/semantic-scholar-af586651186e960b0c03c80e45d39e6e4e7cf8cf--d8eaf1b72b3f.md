## Abstract

Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) has significant advancements in the last few decades. They are designed to convert spoken language into text, providing an interface for various applications. This study investigates the development of a low-resource Sinhala ASR system using transfer learning with state-of-the-art pre-trained multilingual models, Whisper, Wav2Vec2-XLSR, and Wav2Vec2-BERT. Given the scarcity of large annotated datasets for Sinhala, a low-resource language, we utilized a 40-hour speech corpus and to address the limitations of low-resource settings, we leverage transfer learning by fine-tuning pre-trained models on our Sinhala dataset. Our experiments show that the Wav2Vec2-BERT model significantly outperforms previous Sinhala ASR systems, achieving a Word Error Rate (WER) of 1.79% and a Character Error Rate (CER) of 0.33%, without relying on external language models. These results demonstrate the potential of transfer learning for building accurate ASR systems in low-resource settings and establish a new benchmark for Sinhala speech recognition.
