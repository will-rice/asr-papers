## Abstract

Speech recognition technology is one of the key technologies to realize efficient human-computer interaction. As technology continues to advance, end-to-end (E2E) speech recognition has significantly improved processing efficiency and achieved remarkable results. within this study, we review the evolution of speech recognition technology and provide an in-depth introduction to three mainstream E2E speech recognition models: connection timing classification (CTC) model, focus-driven encoder-decoder (AED) model, and RNN Transducer (RNN-T) model, It further analyzes the current research status and challenges within the domain of E2E speech recognition, and offers a perspective on the future development directions of E2E speech recognition technology.
