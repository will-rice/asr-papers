## 1 Introduction

There exists a phenomenon that when human learn to speak one language, they absorb knowledge from mono-lingual resources while they can speak in a code-switching fashion like two or more languages mixed in one sentence consequentially. Inspired by this, a code-switching ASR system can also digested potential acoustics and linguistics information from mono-lingual data.

In recent years, Semi-Supervised Learning (SSL) has been attached a lot of research interests in the fields of Automatic Speech Recognition . The Code-switching ASR is less studied due to the fact that natural code-switching data is quite limited and low-resourced.

This work leverages rich-resourced mono-lingual data to help improve the performances in code-switching scenarios.
